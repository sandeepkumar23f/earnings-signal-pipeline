# Infosys Limited (INFY) — Q2 FY27 Concall Summary

**Call Date:** 2026-10-28 | **BSE Scrip:** `500209` | **Source:** [NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/Infosys_28102026173824_SE_filing_Earnings_call_transcript.pdf) | **Tone:** `Cautiously Optimistic`

## TL;DR
- Revenue growth of 1.5% YoY and 1.2% QoQ in constant currency; full-year revenue guidance revised to 1%-2% YoY.
- AI revenue reached 10.5% of total revenue, growing 29% sequentially, serving as a key secular growth driver.
- Strong large deal wins of $5.2 bn, including a $2 bn net-new mega deal expected to contribute >1% revenue growth in FY28.
- Operating margins stable at 21%; guidance maintained at 20%-22% despite compensation hikes and competitive pricing pressure.
- Management remains disciplined on contract selection, prioritizing economic thresholds over volume growth in a competitive environment.

## Management Commentary
- AI is central to enterprise transformation; Topaz Fabric is enabling token optimization and value realization for clients.
- Large deal pipeline remains healthy with multiple mega deals; focus is on business transformation deals underpinned by AI.
- Competitive intensity is high, particularly regarding AI-led productivity commitments, leading to pricing pressure.
- Hiring remains robust with 4,400 net headcount increase and 8,000 freshers hired in Q2; full-year target of 20,000 remains.
- Offshoring and the decision not to pursue a specific European manufacturing deal continue to impact overall growth guidance by ~2%.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Revenue growth guidance revised to 1% to 2% YoY in constant currency terms.
- Operating margin guidance maintained at 20% to 22% for the year.
- Effective tax rate expected to be in the range of 29% to 30%.

## Segment / Vertical Performance
- **Financial Services:** Robust large deal signings of >$3.5 bn; growth momentum in global/regional banks, cards, and payments.
- **Retail:** Sequential growth impacted by lower third-party revenues and soft consumer demand leading to client ramp-downs.
- **Hi-Tech:** Impacted by lower third-party revenues and reduced spend in a few large clients due to cost pressures.
- **Manufacturing:** Facing near-term pressures including lower revenue from a large client; Middle East conflict and tariffs constraining spend.

## Notable Analyst Q&A
### Q (Meghna Sen, CNBC-TV18): Why was the revenue guidance narrowed despite healthy TCV?
> **Management:** Guidance was revised due to softer-than-expected H1 performance, pricing pressure from competitive intensity, and lower discretionary spend in Retail and Hi-Tech.

### Q (Mansee Dave, ET Now): Is AI monetization fast enough to offset pricing pressure in traditional services?
> **Management:** AI growth is exceptional and secular; while AI productivity impacts traditional billable work, the focus is on scaling AI-led transformation deals.

### Q (Keith Bachman, BMO & Company): Are you seeing in-sourcing trends due to AI?
> **Management:** No pattern of increased in-sourcing due to AI; clients continue to engage Infosys to execute tasks more efficiently using AI.

### Q (Mansee Dave, ET Now): How do you balance hiring 20,000 freshers with margin discipline?
> **Management:** Hiring is a function of attrition and utilization; we maintain 85% utilization and use freshers to backfill attrition, maintaining margin through operational rigor.

## Risks & Concerns Flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and inflationary pressures impacting client discretionary spending.
- Pricing pressure due to high competitive intensity and client demands for AI-led productivity.
- Potential for further macro deterioration impacting the lower end of revenue guidance.
- Seasonally weaker H2 due to furloughs and fewer working days.
