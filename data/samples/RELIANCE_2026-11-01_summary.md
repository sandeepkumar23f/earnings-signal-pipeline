# Reliance Industries Limited (RELIANCE) — Q2 FY2027 Concall Summary

**Call Date:** 2026-11-01 | **BSE Scrip:** `500325` | **Source:** [NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/kavinavora_01112026230907_SE_01112026.pdf) | **Tone:** `Bullish`

## TL;DR
- Consolidated EBITDA reached an all-time high of Rs. 62,409 Cr, up 24% YoY, driven by strong O2C performance and Jio growth.
- O2C segment delivered record EBITDA of Rs. 25,203 Cr (+69% YoY) benefiting from tight energy markets and operational flexibility.
- Jio Platforms reported 18% higher EBITDA at Rs. 22,200 Cr with 542 million subscribers and 300 million 5G users.
- Retail EBITDA margin at 7.8% reflects a conscious strategy to invest in digital commerce infrastructure, with grocery online revenue up 42% YoY.
- New Energy complex remains on track for revenue contribution starting in FY 2028.

## Management Commentary
- O2C performance validated by structural supply issues and operational excellence in crude sourcing despite elevated OpEx and freight costs.
- Jio Platforms sees visibility of high teen growth driven by customer base expansion and digital service layering.
- Retail strategy remains focused on long-term digital commerce scale, trading off short-term earnings for future value creation in FY 2028-29.
- FMCG business achieved 1.6x YoY revenue growth in Q2, with focus on manufacturing and distribution expansion.
- JioStar digital business reached 440 million MAU, with monetization improving through the quarter.

## Guidance & Outlook
- H1 FY 2027 performance tracking toward annualized EBITDA well over Rs. 80,000 Cr for O2C.
- New Energy earnings contribution expected to start in FY 2028.
- Retail business expects margin accretion and benefits of scale to reflect in absolute EBITDA from next year.
- BESS facility commissioning expected in next calendar half-year, with cell production ramping up late next year.

## Segment / Vertical Performance
- **O2C:** EBITDA up 69% YoY to Rs. 25,203 Cr; benefited from tight fuel markets and flexible crude sourcing from Americas/Med regions.
- **Jio Platforms:** Revenue Rs. 40,475 Cr (+11.4% YoY); EBITDA Rs. 21,879 Cr (+16.6% YoY); 5G subscriber base reached 300 million.
- **Reliance Retail:** Revenue grew 6.4% YoY; EBITDA Rs. 6,228 Cr; online grocery revenue up 42% YoY; 303 new stores opened.
- **FMCG:** Revenue Rs. 8,266 Cr (1.6x YoY); beverages led by Campa delivered Rs. 5,250 Cr in H1.
- **Exploration & Production:** Steady quarter with KG-D6 production sustained at 25 million standard cubic meters; natural decline offset by elevated prices.

## Notable Analyst Q&A
### Q (Probal Sen (ICICI Securities)): Probal Sen (ICICI Securities) asked about the timeline for normalization of the 5 million barrels/day of impacted refining capacity.
> **Management:** Management noted high uncertainty and that normalization is not expected immediately given ongoing geopolitical tensions.

### Q (Jal Irani (Nuvama)): Jal Irani (Nuvama) asked for a bifurcation of O2C profit growth drivers.
> **Management:** Management attributed it to a combined effect of petrochemical margins, diesel crack spreads, and the benefit of the TC fleet mitigating freight costs.

### Q (Sanjay Mookim (J.P. Morgan)): Sanjay Mookim (J.P. Morgan) asked for an update on New Energy commissioning.
> **Management:** Solar module capacity is 6+ GW (target 20 GW early next year); BESS facility commissioning expected in next calendar half-year.

### Q (Sachin Salgaonkar (Bank of America)): Sachin Salgaonkar (Bank of America) asked about the timeline for retail margin recovery.
> **Management:** Management expects benefits to start accruing in absolute EBITDA from next year as infrastructure investments scale.

## Risks & Concerns Flagged
- Geopolitical instability in the Middle East and Russia impacting crude supply and freight costs.
- Intense competition in the online retail/quick commerce space leading to margin pressure.
- Inflationary pressures impacting consumer purchasing power and discretionary demand.
- Uncertainty regarding Chinese export quotas and structural demand shifts toward EVs.
