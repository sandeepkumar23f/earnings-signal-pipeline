# Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited (SUNPHARMA) — Q2 FY27 Concall Summary

**Call Date:** 2026-11-04 | **BSE Scrip:** `524715` | **Source:** [NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/SUNPHARMA456_04112026191520_IntimationSE20261104EarningsCallTranscriptQ2FY27_Signed.pdf) | **Tone:** `Cautiously Optimistic`

## TL;DR
- Consolidated revenue grew 11.8% YoY to INR 1,61,000 million, with EBITDA margins at 29.3%.
- US business declined 8.2% YoY due to lenalidomide erosion, though QoQ growth was 6.5%.
- India business remains a strong performer with 17.3% growth, driven by volume expansion and new product launches.
- Organon acquisition remains on track for closure in Q4 FY27; integration planning is progressing as scheduled.
- Ilumya growth slowed in the US; management has implemented interventions and expects recovery over the next few quarters.

## Management Commentary
- Gross margin improved to 80.8% due to favorable product mix and currency tailwinds.
- Ilumya received US approval for psoriatic arthritis; soft launch planned for November.
- India business maintains #1 rank with 8.6% market share; volume growth of 6.7% significantly outperformed IPM growth of 1.8%.
- R&D spend was 4.8% of sales (INR 7,784 million), with management expecting a ramp-up in H2 FY27.
- Voluntary recall of 11 ophthalmic products from the Assam plant initiated to ensure quality and safety.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Maintain high single-digit top-line growth guidance for FY27.
- R&D spend expected to ramp up in H2 FY27 to meet full-year guidance targets.
- Organon acquisition on track to close by Q4 FY27.

## Segment / Vertical Performance
- **India Formulations:** Revenue of INR 55,523 million, up 17.3% YoY; volume growth of 6.7% driven by strong prescription performance.
- **US Business:** Revenue of USD 455 million, down 8.2% YoY due to lenalidomide erosion; 5 new generic products launched in Q2.
- **Global Innovative Medicines:** Sales up 6.9% to USD 356 million; growth impacted by US Ilumya slowdown, partially offset by international performance.
- **Emerging Markets:** Revenue of USD 350 million, up 7.6% YoY; growth driven by branded generics and Ilumya.

## Notable Analyst Q&A
### Q (Dr. Kunal Dhamesha, Macquarie Capital): What is driving the slowdown in US Ilumya growth and what is the recovery timeline?
> **Management:** Management identified execution factors and market dynamics; interventions are in place, and the new psoriatic arthritis indication is expected to return the product to typical growth patterns over the next few quarters.

### Q (Dr. Kunal Dhamesha, Macquarie Capital): Is the 17% growth in India business sustainable and what is the volume vs. price mix?
> **Management:** Growth is broad-based across 12 therapy areas; 50-60% of growth is driven by volume and new products, with the remainder from pricing.

### Q (Juhi Kumari, Narnolia Financial Services): Will the Organon acquisition involve further exceptional expenses in coming quarters?
> **Management:** Yes, acquisition-related costs will continue until the transaction closes in Q4 FY27, with some costs being back-ended.

### Q (Vishal Manchanda, Systematix): Can you comment on the media report regarding the Assam plant ophthalmic products?
> **Management:** The company voluntarily recalled 11 products and initiated a temporary cessation of manufacturing to ensure quality and safety; the regulator has been kept informed.

## Risks & Concerns Flagged
- US market generic erosion (specifically lenalidomide).
- Execution risks in the US specialty business.
- Regulatory and quality compliance risks (e.g., Assam plant recall).
- Uncertainty in R&D clinical trial outcomes and timelines.
- Integration risks associated with the Organon acquisition.
