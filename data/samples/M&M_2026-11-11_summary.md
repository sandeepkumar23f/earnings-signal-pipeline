# Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (M&M) — Q2 FY27 Concall Summary

**Call Date:** 2026-11-11 | **BSE Scrip:** `500520` | **Source:** [NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/AmitDodani_11112026185839_Intimation_Transcript_Q2F27_Earningscall_05112026.pdf) | **Tone:** `Bullish`

## TL;DR
- Consolidated revenue and PAT both grew 25% YoY, with YTD profits up 29% despite significant commodity inflation.
- Auto business remains capacity-constrained with strong demand; capacity expansion to 82k units/month by year-end and 132k by F31.
- Farm sector outlook revised to 'flattish' for the full year due to monsoon deficits and international market slowdowns.
- Growth Gems (Logistics, Accelo, Mahindra Finance) and Tech M are delivering strong profitability, reducing reliance on Auto/Farm alone.
- Management reaffirmed long-term commitments of 15-20% EPS growth and 18% RoE.

## Management Commentary
- Resilience was the key theme, with proactive pricing, hedging, and product mix actions offsetting commodity inflation.
- International Farm subsidiaries are being restructured; exits from Sampo and Erkunt Foundry are complete, with Mitsubishi Mahindra Ag Machinery closure expected by Q4.
- EV penetration reached 12% of total SUV sales; 59 ultra-fast charging stations are operational with a target of 250 by end of next year.
- Aerospace business is scaling rapidly with a target of $1 billion in orders per year by F30, driven by Airbus and Boeing contracts.
- Five major product launches planned by June 2027, including new NU_IQ platform products and LCVs.

## Guidance & Outlook
- Auto capacity: 82k units/month by end of F27, 92k by F28, and 132k by F31.
- Farm industry outlook: Revised from 5% growth to 'flattish' for the full year.
- SUV outlook: Mid to high teens growth.
- LCV outlook: Increased to double-digit growth for the industry.
- Long-term targets: 15-20% EPS growth and 18% RoE.

## Segment / Vertical Performance
- **Auto:** Revenue up 55%; strong demand across SUV and LCV portfolios; constrained by capacity.
- **Farm:** Revenue down 9% due to season shift, monsoon deficit, and international demand slowdown.
- **Mahindra Finance:** AUM grew to over ₹1,30,000 crore; strong operational performance driven by tech and data.
- **Tech M:** Profits up 33% with large deal wins and strong pipeline.
- **Aerospace:** Order book grew 28X; recently won major fuselage contract for Airbus A320neo family.

## Notable Analyst Q&A
### Q (Yogesh, HSBC): Are EVs driven by fuel prices, and will demand pause if crude falls?
> **Management:** Demand is driven by total cost of ownership savings and improved user experience; the 'tunneling effect' has crossed barrier resistance, making it a sustainable trend.

### Q (Kapil, Nomura): Why not think bigger on capacity given consistent shortages?
> **Management:** We are doubling capacity with the new Nagpur plant and have acquired 1,500-2,000 acres of land to allow for rapid future expansion.

### Q (Binay, Analyst): How do you see the mix of sub-4m vs. larger SUVs impacting margins?
> **Management:** We position products based on value rather than length; sub-4m products like Thar and 3XO are highly aspirational and maintain healthy margins.

### Q (Rakesh, Analyst): Is the tractor industry facing inventory clearing risks?
> **Management:** Inventory is not abnormally high compared to past downcycles; we are factoring some correction into our estimates, but retail demand is not as weak as the -10% volume projection suggests.

## Risks & Concerns Flagged
- Significant commodity inflation (Steel +17%, Copper +42%, Rubber +40%).
- Monsoon deficit impacting rural demand and tractor sales.
- Capacity constraints limiting growth in high-demand SUV and LCV segments.
- Geopolitical issues and international market slowdowns affecting global subsidiaries.
